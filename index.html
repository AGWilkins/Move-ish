WHERE DID THIS COME FROM?

Move-ish escaped from Clarity Cycle.

Clarity Cycle is a series of small daily experiments in attention, behaviour and better decision-making.

It started during the Health cycle with a simple thought: what if we moved more often, but stopped making such a bloody production out of exercise?

Then AI got involved.

What followed became an experiment in human–AI co-creation — ideas, music, humour and code bouncing backwards and forwards until something neither side had quite imagined appeared.

The result was this.
